# Parity audit: `calib3d` ↔ OpenCV 4.10.0

Task B5 of [#172](https://github.com/webarkit/purecv/issues/172), sub-issue
[#177](https://github.com/webarkit/purecv/issues/177). Method, statuses (`measured`,
`inspected`, `diverges`, `intentional`, `purecv-only`, `missing`) and dimensions D1–D7:
[`README.md`](README.md). Inventory taken at the branch base `ecc29ad` (`parity-audit` after
the video audit, PR #255): every `pub` item of `src/calib3d` (`src/calib3d.rs` and the public
submodules `fundamental`, `geometry`, `homography`, `pose`, `undistort`), the variants of the
three public enums, and the re-exports of `src/calib3d.rs` and of the crate prelude. The
module has no public inherent methods, traits, constants or statics; `src/calib3d/linalg.rs`
and `src/calib3d/simd.rs` are `pub(crate)` (the latter is empty). The wasm wrappers
(`crates/wasm/src/lib.rs`: `findHomography`, `solvePnP`, `solvePnPRansac`, `Rodrigues`) call
these items and are not audited separately, except that their `map_solve_pnp_method` carries
the discriminant finding of `SolvePnPMethod` below.

Headline findings, in order of impact (details in the rows, issues in
[Proposed issues](#proposed-issues)):

1. **What matches OpenCV 4.10.0.** `rodrigues` in the vector-to-matrix direction (25 vectors
   from 1e-12 to beyond 2π, to 1 ulp) and in the matrix-to-vector direction for proper
   rotations (to 1.5e-8, including OpenCV's sign rule at π and the reduction of angles beyond
   π). `solve_pnp` with `Iterative` on non-planar points, exact and noisy (2e-8: Gauss-Newton
   and OpenCV's Levenberg-Marquardt reach the same minimum from the same DLT start).
   `solve_pnp_ransac` on 24 points with 6 random outliers: the inlier index sets are OpenCV's
   18 true inliers and the poses agree to 2e-8. `init_undistort_rectify_map` with 4, 5 and 8
   coefficients, with and without a rectification and a new camera matrix: the `float32` maps
   are byte-identical (FNV-1a digests). `find_fundamental_mat` with `FM_8POINT`: identical
   normalisation, eigen solution and rank-2 projection, matrices equal to 3e-13 on exact,
   noisy and outlier-ridden sets. `find_homography` on exact correspondences (2.6e-6, every
   method) and the RANSAC/LMedS inlier sets on 10 gross outliers (OpenCV's 30 true inliers).
   The `HomographyMethod` and `FundamentalMatMethod` values.
2. **`solve_pnp` is wrong on coplanar object points.** OpenCV's `SOLVEPNP_ITERATIVE`
   detects a planar structure and initialises from a homography; purecv's 12-parameter DLT is
   rank deficient there and the returned pose is 2.9 rad and 8 units off on an exact 16-point
   planar target (the common AR case). The same function ignores `dist_coeffs` (0.05 units
   off with a 5-coefficient model), needs 6 points where OpenCV solves 4 planar points or 3
   with a guess, and `SolvePnPMethod::SqPnP = 6` is OpenCV's `SOLVEPNP_IPPE` (`SQPNP = 8`);
   every method but `Iterative` returns `NotImplemented`. `use_extrinsic_guess` → #133, the
   optimiser → #135.
3. **`find_homography` stops at the DLT.** OpenCV refines every result of more than four
   points (methods 0, RANSAC, LMEDS) with ten Levenberg-Marquardt iterations on the
   reprojection error (0.009–0.011 difference on noisy data here); `LMedS` and `Rho` run
   purecv's RANSAC; a non-positive threshold fails instead of meaning 3; collinear or
   coincident points return a matrix with a zero last row instead of an empty result; the
   RANSAC mask is recomputed from the refit instead of being the best minimal model's.
4. **`find_fundamental_mat`'s robust path is another estimator.** OpenCV samples 7 points
   (1–3 solutions each), scores with the larger point-to-epiline distance, keeps the best
   minimal model without a refit, runs LMedS whenever `FM_RANSAC` has fewer than 15 points,
   and resets a non-positive threshold to 3 and an out-of-range confidence to 0.99; purecv
   samples 8 points, scores with the Sampson distance, refits the inliers and masks from the
   refit. On exact data every point is an inlier in both; on noisy and outlier sets the masks
   differ by two to eight points (the extra purecv inliers on the outlier set are two true
   outliers within 3 px of their epilines). Seven points and degenerate sets are errors or
   garbage instead of a 7-point solution set or an empty matrix.
5. **`rodrigues` edge cases.** Matrices with a rotation angle below 1e-5 rad come back as the
   zero vector in OpenCV and as the angle in purecv (`keep?`); for `1e-5 ≤ π − θ < 1e-4`
   purecv's diagonal method leaves a 3.1e-5 spurious component where OpenCV's general formula
   is exact; a matrix that is not a rotation is projected onto one (`U·Vᵀ`) by OpenCV and read
   as is by purecv; a 1x1 three-channel vector is rejected; the Jacobian output is missing.
6. **`init_undistort_rectify_map`** ignores the thin-prism (`s1..s4`) and tilt
   (`τx, τy`) coefficients (0.18 px and 4.2 px off), accepts 3-, 6- or 9-element vectors
   that OpenCV rejects, requires a new camera matrix where OpenCV defaults to the camera matrix
   with a centred principal point, and panics on a 0x0 size in the `parallel` build.

What matches, in full: `HomographyMethod` (0, 4, 8, 16), `FundamentalMatMethod` (2, 8), the
implemented `SolvePnPMethod` values (0, 1, 2, 5), `find_homography` on exact data and with
four points, its outlier inlier sets, its too-few-points and length checks; `rodrigues` as
above and its shape checks; `solve_pnp` and `solve_pnp_ransac` on non-planar data, their
point-count and length checks, the `dist_coeffs = None` path; `init_undistort_rectify_map` as
above, the 1x1 size and the 3x1 `R` rejection; `find_fundamental_mat` `FM_8POINT` on 8, 10 and
60 points, its RANSAC inlier sets on exact data (every point), the six-point and length checks.

## Notes

- **Counting.** The summary counts one row per table line (59 rows; grouped `missing` lines
  count once). The three rows marked `measured` / `diverges` (the `Ransac` inlier set,
  `rodrigues` input validation, the `solve_pnp` point count) are counted once each, as
  `diverges`, inside the 25.
- **D6.** Neither `parallel` nor `simd` changed any result (byte-identical dumps for the four
  builds); the 0x0 panic is `parallel`-only. `wasm32` was not run.
- **Tolerances.** `tests/parity_calib3d.rs` enforces 1e-5 on homographies (`H[2][2] = 1`;
  measured 2.6e-6, RHO 7.3e-6; four points 2.5e-5 at 1e-4), 1e-15 / 1e-7 on Rodrigues
  matrices / vectors (measured 4.4e-16 / 1.5e-8), 1e-6 on poses (measured 2e-8), exact
  digests on the undistortion maps (1e-12 on the zero-coefficient samples), 1e-10 on
  fundamental matrices (measured 2.8e-13; RANSAC on exact data 3.1e-6 at 1e-5).
- **Panics.** One found: `init_undistort_rectify_map` with a 0x0 size under `parallel`
  ([#264](https://github.com/webarkit/purecv/issues/264)). None on the measured inputs otherwise.
- **IPP / optimisations.** Nothing here runs through IPP. The dispatched
  `initUndistortRectifyMapComputer` changed no value with non-zero coefficients (D7 record).
- **Not measurable with `cv2`.** `LMSolver` (not bound); OpenCV's internal RANSAC
  iterations (only the final mask and model are exposed).
- **Cross-module dependencies.** `solve_pnp`'s distortion path needs `undistortPoints`
  (`missing`); `undistort` needs `imgproc::remap`
  ([#237](https://github.com/webarkit/purecv/issues/237)); the RANSAC rows need `cv::RNG`
  ([#225](https://github.com/webarkit/purecv/issues/225)); `find_homography`'s and
  `solve_pnp`'s refinements, `calibrateCamera` and `solvePnPRefineLM` need the LM solver
  ([#135](https://github.com/webarkit/purecv/issues/135)); `rodrigues`'s projection step can
  reuse the private `svd_3x3` (`core.md`: `SVDecomp` is `missing`); the pattern detectors
  need `corner_sub_pix` ([#245](https://github.com/webarkit/purecv/issues/245)). Nothing here
  uses `fastAtan2` ([#250](https://github.com/webarkit/purecv/issues/250)) or the shared
  `saturate_cast` ([#189](https://github.com/webarkit/purecv/issues/189)).
- **Inputs.** The scripts and the tests build every input from the same integer generator
  (a 32-bit LCG; jitter `k/64`, coordinates `k/1024`, noise `±0.5 · k/1024`, outliers as
  integer pixels), exact Pythagorean rotations (`5-12-13`, `3-4-5`, `8-15-17`, `7-24-25`,
  `9-40-41`) multiplied in a fixed order, and the pinhole projection in a fixed order, so
  every `f32` point is bit-identical on both sides (digests checked); only the distorted
  projections come from OpenCV and are embedded.
